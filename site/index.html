Proven elsewhere
48 days
sooner into stable housing
New Leaf Project · BC
Dive in

50 people who had recently lost housing each received a one-time $7,500. They moved into stable housing in 96 days on average, against 144 for the comparison group. Foundations for Social Change with UBC; peer-reviewed in PNAS, 2023.

HOPE will pay for essentials and restoration costs directly rather than hand over cash. The lesson carries: resources plus trust move people forward.

Questions worth asking

Clear before you commit.

HOPE is ambitious and still in development. These are the straight answers today. Confirmed partner, funding and launch details will replace development language as agreements close.

See the trust commitments Information for members Read the privacy notice
What is the HOPE Card?

A closed-loop program access card, in development. It is not a bank or credit product. The chip and card format make it familiar and visible; the proposed model would open approved essentials at participating vendors and provider-paid Restoration Track steps.

Can I donate right now?

Not through secure checkout yet. The Stripe environment is prepared, but it will activate only when the receiving charity and fund-stewardship pathway are confirmed. Until then, the giving page records your intended amount so the HOPE team can follow up.

Will I receive a charitable tax receipt?

The receiving-charity pathway is in process. Before any payment, the giving page will name that charity and state whether an official receipt is available. An eligible receipt will come from that receiving charity, not HOPE Tech Inc. Read the full commitment.

Are Hamilton organizations already official partners?

Community conversations are active, but HOPE will not present a conversation as an endorsement. Organizations will be named only after an agreement is finalized and permission to publish the relationship is clear.

Where will the money go?

The proposed model uses one stewarded Hamilton pool for approved daily essentials, pilot infrastructure and verified Restoration Track steps. The final receiving entity, uses, fees and settlement process will be published before live partner-processed giving opens.

How is member privacy protected?

Donors should see pooled impact and honest outcomes, never names, daily purchase histories or private case notes. A member controls whether and how their story is shared. The public contact form is separate from future member systems. Read the current privacy notice.

When does the Hamilton pilot launch?

The first card-supported essentials are targeted for the first half of 2027. That is a target, not a promise: funding, signed partner agreements, safeguarding, vendor readiness and a confirmed receiving charity must all be in place first.

How can a business or community organization help?

Vendors can register interest in approved redemptions, service providers can help shape Restoration Track supports, and community organizations can discuss referrals, advocates, safeguarding and fund stewardship. No conversation is presented as a partnership before terms and permission are confirmed. Choose the relevant pathway in the form below and we will come back with real terms.

Is this surveillance? Can anyone track a member?

The proposed system is designed to prevent donor access to a member's name, vendor, location or purchase history. HOPE will collect only what is necessary to operate support safely. Information may still be disclosed when consent is given, a child-protection duty applies, serious imminent harm must be addressed, or disclosure is legally compelled. Those limits will be explained before participation.

Does someone have to earn help, or perform progress?

No. Food, transit, clothing and personal care are unconditional and stay that way whether or not a person engages, makes progress, shares a story or wants a relationship. Larger commitments like rent or tuition are planned with the member and approved by a qualified professional, because those are decisions with consequences, not because someone has to prove they deserve them. A member can take a card and never speak to us again.

HOPE is faith-rooted. Does that change who gets help?

No. Anyone can receive and anyone can give. Faith-rooted support is offered only to people who want it and is never attached to essentials, the plan or the Restoration Track. Larger restoration steps would be approved under published program criteria by an appropriately qualified professional, not by a donor or on the basis of faith.

Is this paternalistic?

That risk is real whenever one group designs support for another. HOPE's answer is to keep essentials unconditional, let the member refuse without penalty, write the plan together and give donors no control over an individual. The member-facing rules and complaint path will be tested with people who have lived experience before launch.

Are you replacing services, or privatizing them?

No. HOPE is being designed to complement public and community services, not replace them. Hamilton's coordinated-access process remains the housing pathway; any future HOPE support would work around that process and never claim authority over it.
